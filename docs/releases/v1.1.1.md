# Sweep 1.1.1

Small follow-up to 1.1.0.

## Fixed

- Scan failures caused by macOS privacy protection now offer a direct **Open Full Disk Access…** button next to Retry. This is the case for `~/.Trash`, which macOS only exposes to apps granted Full Disk Access — without it, Sweep could not list your Trash and used to report it as empty.

After granting Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, rescan the category and your Trash contents will appear.

## Install

1. Download `Sweep-1.1.1.dmg`
2. Open it and drag **Sweep** into **Applications**
